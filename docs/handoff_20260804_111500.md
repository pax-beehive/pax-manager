# Business Turn Lifecycle Handoff

## Summary

Conversation streaming and durable session history now share a stable business
`turn_id`. Clients can use that ID to own live output per turn and replace it
with durable history after the turn completes. No ACP request ID, transport
sequence, or reliable queue sequence is used as the business turn ID.

## Business Turn ID Flow

- A direct conversation prompt receives a new `turn_*` ID before the ACP prompt
  is sent.
- A queued follow-up keeps the distinct `queued_turn_id` allocated by the queue
  API and uses it when that turn starts.
- The business ID travels inside manager runtime and history projection context.
  It is not added as a transport sequence or replay cursor.
- Runtime `ActiveTurnID` contains the business ID while the turn is active and is
  cleared when the prompt completes or fails.

## Conversation SSE Lifecycle

- `turn_started` is emitted before visible output for a new turn.
- Turn-scoped `acp`, `approval_required`, `interrupted`, error, and `turn_done`
  envelopes carry the same business `turn_id`.
- `turn_done` is emitted once for each completed business turn with
  `status=complete`.
- The existing `done` envelope remains scoped to the whole conversation HTTP
  request or queued chain. It has no `turn_id` and is not a turn completion
  signal.

Permission interruption pauses the active turn without emitting `turn_done`.
Permission resume emits `turn_started` with `status=resumed` and continues with
the original business `turn_id`. A queued follow-up starts only after the prior
turn completes and uses its own ID.

## Durable History Boundary

User prompts, agent message and thought chunks, tool updates, permission frames,
and derived Pax invocation or artifact displays inherit the active business
`turn_id`.

Before conversation SSE emits `turn_done`, the manager:

1. Flushes pending history text batches.
2. Upserts a durable completion message with:
   - `message_type=turn_done`
   - `status=complete`
   - the same business `turn_id`
   - `raw_json.turn_status=complete`
3. Emits conversation SSE `turn_done`.

The regular authenticated session history endpoint returns this marker. A client
may refetch history after `turn_done` and replace the matching live overlay with
durable messages for that `turn_id`.

## Observer Protocol

The session observer protocol is unchanged. `session_observer.go` has no diff
from the base branch. It does not emit `turn_started`; its existing `acp` and
`turn_done` envelopes retain their existing shape and `turn_done.status=done`.
They naturally use the business `ActiveTurnID`. Observer replay and `after_seq`
remain out of scope.

## Known Risks

- A paused session created before this change may not have a business turn ID.
  Resume allocates one, but already-written untagged history is not backfilled.
- Observer replay is intentionally not implemented.
- The queued-turn registry retains its existing process-local persistence
  behavior.
